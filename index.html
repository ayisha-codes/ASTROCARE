Health Guidance

  • Review changes by comparing today's entry with your previous entries.
  • Add notes whenever something feels different, even if it seems small.
  • Contact the crew medical officer if you feel unwell or concerned.
  • Alert the crew and follow mission medical protocols for urgent or worsening symptoms.

AstroCare does not diagnose or decide what is normal for you. Your medical team does.

Space Hazards Awareness

NASA's Human Research Program groups spaceflight health risks into hazard areas. These are brief, general summaries.

☢

Space Radiation

Beyond Earth's protective magnetic field, astronauts are exposed to more radiation. Over long missions this is a concern for long-term health, so exposure is carefully tracked.

☉

Altered Gravity

Living in microgravity affects the body, including muscles, bones, and the cardiovascular system. Exercise and medical monitoring help the crew adapt.

☀

Isolation and Confinement

Small crews, limited space, and distance from home can affect mood, sleep, and teamwork. Noticing changes early supports both well-being and performance.

☁

Hostile, Closed Environments

A spacecraft is a sealed habitat. Air quality, noise, temperature, and cleanliness all shape crew health and need ongoing attention.

Learn more: NASA Human Research Program, "Risks of Human Spaceflight" (nasa.gov/hrp).

About AstroCare

The problem

On long missions, astronauts are far from hospitals and can face delays in contacting Earth. Small day-to-day changes in health and mood are easy to miss without a simple record.

How AstroCare helps

AstroCare gives astronauts a quick daily check-in, a clear history of their entries, and trend charts that make changes easy to see. It also reminds them when to speak with the crew medical officer.

Current limitations

  • Prototype only; not a medical device and not clinically validated.
  • Sample values are illustrative, not real astronaut measurements.
  • Data is manually entered and stored only in this browser (localStorage).
  • No diagnosis, no alert thresholds, no medical-team connection.

Future possibilities

  • Integration with validated wearable sensors.
  • Secure review of entries by the medical team.
  • Multilingual support for international crews.
